🎲 Statistical simulation (Monte Carlo)

Every input is randomized at once, 1,000 times, within the uncertainty band you choose (a uniform distribution around each input's current value) — showing the realistic spread of the outcome instead of one best-guess number, plus which inputs actually drive that spread when everything moves together.

🧮 Statistical tests

A flexible data table for hypothesis testing, chi-square, and one-way/two-way ANOVA — fill it from your build below, or type in your own numbers. Group A is required for every test; Group B is only needed for chi-square's test of independence and for two-way ANOVA.

ValueGroup AGroup B

Hypothesis test (t-test)

Chi-square test

Uses Group A × Group B counts (test of independence) if Group B is filled in on any row, otherwise Group A counts against a uniform expectation (goodness-of-fit).

One-way ANOVA

Compares means across every distinct Group A value.

Two-way ANOVA

Needs Group B filled in on every row, with the same number of rows (at least 2) in every Group A × Group B combination — a balanced design.

📈 Chart builder

Freeform plotting — add any series of numbers, pick bar/line/scatter, choose its color, and edit its legend label. "Load from data table" turns each Group A above into its own series.

Data sources

About this data: eco-cost figures are based on Idematapp / ecocostsvalue.com reference data (2020 excerpts), covering common materials, processes, energy, and transport modes — use the "Custom line item" field for anything not listed. Method: single-indicator eco-costs (euros), the prevention-cost approach.

About the carbon / water / energy / recycled-content figures: these are representative reference values compiled from general public LCA literature, not from the same certified source as the eco-cost figures above. Treat them as indicative, and use the custom line fields for measured or certified numbers where precision matters.
